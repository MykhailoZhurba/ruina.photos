Privacy

This website is the photography portfolio of Mykhailo Zhurba (Ruina Photos). This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your data when you visit it.

What this website collects

Nothing that identifies you. The site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it has no forms, accounts or comments. Fonts and scripts are served from the site itself, so your browser does not contact other companies just to display it.

Hosting

Like any website, the web host that serves these pages may keep technical server logs, such as your IP address, the page you requested and the time. This is handled by the hosting provider under its own privacy policy, and I do not receive or use this information.

Links to other sites

The Instagram and Facebook icons are ordinary links. Those services only receive information about you if you choose to click them, and their own privacy policies then apply.

Photos of people

If you appear in a photo on this site and would like it removed, message me and I will take care of it. All photographs are © Mykhailo Zhurba; please ask before using them.
