About Mykhailo Zhurba

Hey, Ruina Photos here!

I’m Mykhailo Zhurba, a photographer working in Latvia. I’ve been shooting for a few years, with my main focus on automotive and portrait photography. My goal is to capture the beauty of the moment and create lasting memories.

What I photograph

  • Automotive: cars at events and on location, plus drift coverage as current media for LatviaDrift.
  • Portraits: portrait and cosplay shoots.
  • Aviation: jets and aircraft at airshows.
  • Aerial: aerial photography for paid projects.
  • Street: old-town streets and everyday city moments.

Work with me

I do paid photoshoots: portrait, automotive and aerial. I’m open to all proposals and offers.

Get in touch

The quickest way to reach me is a message on Instagram or Facebook. Tell me what you have in mind, such as the type of shoot, the date and the place, and I’ll get back to you.
